Building a Better Government Through Documentation & Transparent Reform

FixTheSystem.in is an open public knowledge repository dedicated to documenting how Indian governmental systems currently work, identifying inefficiencies, researching successful international practices, and proposing practical reforms that improve transparency, efficiency, accountability, and public service delivery.

Mission

📚 Document Reality

Create accurate documentation explaining how every public process currently works.

🔍 Identify Problems

Highlight unnecessary complexity, delays, lack of accountability, and opportunities for corruption.

🌍 Research the World

Study systems implemented successfully in countries across Europe, North America, East Asia, and elsewhere.

💡 Propose Better Systems

Design practical, technology-driven improvements that are transparent, scalable, and citizen-centric.

Methodology

1. Document Current System

Understand the complete workflow including every office, approval, document, and stakeholder involved.

2. Identify Bottlenecks

Measure delays, unnecessary approvals, duplicated work, opportunities for bribery, and lack of transparency.

3. Compare Globally

Research how countries with highly efficient public administration solve similar problems.

4. Design Better Processes

Recommend simplified workflows, digital-first services, automation, audit trails, public dashboards, and policy improvements.

5. Open Public Review

Publish proposals for constructive feedback, expert discussion, and continuous improvement.

FixTheSystem.in

Open Documentation • Research • Public Collaboration • Better Governance


This project aims to encourage evidence-based discussion about improving public administration. It documents systems, analyzes processes, and proposes reforms based on research and comparative governance studies.
